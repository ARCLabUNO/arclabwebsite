Congratulations to ARC Lab member Dr. John Ursino on being selected as the University of Nebraska Omaha's nominee in the Social Sciences category for the Council of Graduate Schools/ProQuest Distinguished Dissertation Award.

His dissertation, Crowding, Capacity, and Reform: Exploring the Dynamics of Correctional Policy, examines how correctional systems respond to population pressure and the implications of those responses for correctional reform.
