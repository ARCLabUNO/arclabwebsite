On November 9, 2026, ARC Lab Co-Directors Drs. Jennifer Tostlebe and Zachary Hamilton delivered an invited presentation to the University of Cincinnati Corrections Institute (UCCI).

The presentation highlighted findings from their recent Criminology publication, Criminal History’s Expiration Date, which examines how the predictive value of criminal history diminishes over time. They also discussed ongoing research exploring how these findings can inform more accurate, time-sensitive approaches to correctional risk assessment.
