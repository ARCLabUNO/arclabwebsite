Members of the Applied Research in Corrections (ARC) Lab at the University of Nebraska at Omaha will present their research and receive several prestigious awards at the 2026 American Society of Criminology (ASC) Annual Meeting, November 18–21 in Chicago, Illinois.

Across 11 presentations spanning six sessions, ARC Lab researchers will contribute to discussions on correctional policy, prisoner reentry, risk assessment, institutional environments, and crime prevention. This year's conference will also recognize the contributions of ARC Lab faculty and doctoral students to the field of corrections and sentencing.

We invite conference attendees to join us for our presentations and award celebrations throughout the week!

## Celebrating ARC Lab Research and Scholarship

From examining prison population dynamics and parole decision-making to evaluating community-based reentry programs and crime prevention initiatives, this year's presentations highlight the breadth of research underway at the ARC Lab.

We are especially excited to celebrate the accomplishments of our faculty and doctoral students, whose recognition at this year's conference reflects their contributions to advancing rigorous, policy-relevant corrections research.

**We invite all ASC attendees to join us for these presentations, celebrate our award recipients, and connect with the ARC Lab throughout the conference. We look forward to sharing our research, engaging in meaningful conversations, and seeing everyone in Chicago!**
