ARC Lab Director Jenn Tostlebe has received the *Distinguished New Scholar Award* from the American Society of Criminology’s Division on Corrections and Sentencing.

The Distinguished New Scholar Award recognizes outstanding early-career achievement in corrections and sentencing research, with consideration given to both scholarly contributions and service to the Division.

Notably, Tostlebe was one of two ARC Lab professors recognized by the Division this year, with Zach Hamilton receiving the Distinguished Scholar Award.

The award will be presented at the 2026 American Society of Criminology Annual Meeting in Chicago.
