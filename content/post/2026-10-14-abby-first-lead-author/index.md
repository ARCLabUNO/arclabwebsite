Congratulations to Ph.D. Candidate, [Abby Hayes](https://arcorrectionslab.org/author/abigail-hayes/), on her first lead-authored publication in Crime & Delinquency.

The article is titled ["Beyond misconduct: Experimental and qualitative evidence on daily life, perceptions of correctional officers, and reform in a restrictive housing step-down program."](https://arcorrectionslab.org/publication/hayes_et_al_2026/)
