The University of Nebraska at Omaha recently featured the accomplishments of ARC Lab faculty and students in an article highlighting the lab's recognition by the American Society of Criminology's Division on Corrections and Sentencing.

ARC Lab Co-Directors Zachary Hamilton and Jennifer Tostlebe will receive the Distinguished Scholar Award and Distinguished New Scholar Award, respectively, recognizing their contributions to corrections and sentencing research. Three doctoral students were also recognized: Caitlyn Jordon and Taylor Gonzales received the Ben Steiner Excellence in Corrections Student Paper Award, while Abigail Hayes received the Dr. Kimchi Memorial Graduate Travel Award.

The UNO feature includes reflections from Hamilton and Tostlebe on their research, professional collaborations, and commitment to graduate student mentorship. Together, the five awards highlight an exciting year for the ARC Lab and its ongoing contributions to corrections scholarship.
